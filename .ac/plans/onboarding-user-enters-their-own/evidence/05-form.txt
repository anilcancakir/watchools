=== Screenshot QA (05-form.png), what it caught ===
The first screenshot at /saglayici (chrome, `fsa start` + `dusk:navigate --route
/saglayici` + `dusk:screenshot`) showed "Kaydet" and "Çıkış yap" rendered at the
top-left corner of their pill instead of centred: `items-center justify-center`
on a `WDiv` only take effect on a flex container in real Tailwind/CSS
semantics, and both buttons' className was missing `flex flex-row`. The back
button's arrow looked centred anyway, because Flutter's own `Icon` widget
self-centers its glyph inside a `Center` (`icon.dart:354`) regardless of the
parent's alignment; `WText`/`Text` has no such self-centering, so it exposed
the missing `flex`. Fixed by adding `flex flex-row` to both buttons'
className. Re-ran the widget test suite (still 12/12 green) and re-captured
the screenshot: "Kaydet" now centres correctly. Full test output above is from
after this fix.
