=== 3. The QA assertions, and which test carries each ===

- the fake satisfies the interface with no platform
  "a fake is a PlaybackEngine and reports idle before anything is loaded"
  (no method channel, no widget mount, no clock injection: StallDetector reads
  the tick's own monotonicNs)

- the measured idle-display shape reads as notPresenting rather than stalled
  "notPresenting for the measured idle-display shape, never stalled": frozen
  timePos with underrun false and demuxerIdle true, held at 1 s, 5 s, 20 s and
  60 s, so it is asserted well past the 12 s grace and never accumulates.
  The shape is `player-layer.md:587`, read from the research itself: time-pos
  frozen, underrun **false**, demuxerIdle **true**, fw-bytes to the cap.
  (`research/explore-existing-player-research.md:40` reproduces that row with
  demuxerIdle as "-"; the primary table says true. A sequence scripted from the
  summary would carry demuxerIdle null and classify as stalled, which is why
  this was checked against the measurement rather than the summary.)

- a frozen timePos with underrun true crosses into stalled only after the grace
  "stalled only once the freeze reaches the grace": anchor at 10 s, frozen at
  21.9 s is starving (11.9 s), frozen at 22.0 s is stalled (12.0 s, the
  boundary is >= grace). Plus "starving while frozen and out of data inside the
  grace" at the measured 8 s starvation.

- a session change resets the freeze anchor
  "a new session resets the freeze anchor": starving in session 7, then a load,
  then session 8 advancing at 3 s and frozen at 14 s reads starving (11 s from
  the new anchor) rather than stalled (14 s from the old one).

- a tick from a previous session is not read as the current session's
  "a tick from the load that was replaced is not read as the current one": the
  session-7 straggler arriving after the second load reaches neither health
  (idle) nor the ticks stream (seen == [7, 7, 8]).
