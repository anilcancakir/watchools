Step 9's runtime proof of `http_multiple=0`: ATTEMPTED, NOT ACHIEVED.

This file exists because the attempt failed to discriminate, and a plan that
claimed the option was proved when it was not would be worse than one that says
this plainly.

## What the step asked for

Play the m3u8-only channel and show that no two segment requests overlap in
time, then invert the option, rebuild, and show that they do. Both logs, or it
is not evidence.

## What had to be worked around first, and it is a finding of its own

The app could not reach the HLS demuxer at all. `XtreamStreamUrl.live` takes a
`channelFormats` argument, `Channel` carries no formats field, and
`ProviderSession.streamUrlFor` never passes one, so `served` is always empty,
`allowed` decides alone, and `ts` leads the preference
(`xtream_stream_url.dart:39`). Measured: tapping channel 10007, whose catalogue
entry is `formats: ['m3u8']`, produced

    GET /live/demo/demo/10007.ts

So **step 1's fix is not merely latent for most channels, it is unreachable from
the app as it stands.** The option is still correct and costs nothing; what is
missing is the plumbing that would ever select an m3u8 URL.

To reach the HLS path at all, the mock's account was temporarily narrowed to
`allowed_output_formats: ['m3u8']` (`server.mjs:174`), which is a legitimate
provider configuration rather than a contrivance. That produced real HLS:

    GET /live/demo/demo/10007.m3u8
    GET /live/play/<token>/10007.m3u8
    GET /segments/10007/init.mp4
    GET /segments/10007/s-447275531.m4s
    GET /segments/10007/s-447275532.m4s
    GET /segments/10007/s-447275533.m4s

## The measurement, and why it settles nothing

The mock logs a line per request ARRIVAL and never a completion, so start
timestamps cannot separate "sequential and fast" from "overlapping". The
substitute was a direct count of established TCP connections from the app to the
mock, sampled every two seconds through `lsof`.

    http_multiple=0 (shipped)   2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 2
    http_multiple=1 (control)   1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 1

The two runs are indistinguishable. The count fluctuates in BOTH states, because
a playlist reload and a segment fetch are separate `URLContext`s and briefly
coexist whatever `http_multiple` says.

This is an under-powered experiment rather than a negative result. The mock
serves segments off local disk in single-digit milliseconds, so any overlap
window `http_multiple` would open is far below a two second sampling interval.

## What would settle it

Either instrument the mock to hold each segment response open for a second or
two, which makes an overlap window wide enough to see with the same instrument;
or raise mpv's log level past the `msg-level=all=warn` the engine sets
(`MpvEngine.swift:97`) and read FFmpeg's own option handling back out of the
verbose log. Both are source changes outside this step's scope, and both are
recorded in the plan's Deferred Ideas.

## What IS established

That the two option keys sit on the passthroughs that declare them, checked by
the four greps in step 1, and that the macOS build compiles with them. That the
option is ACCEPTED by FFmpeg rather than silently dropped is the specific claim
this file declines to make.
